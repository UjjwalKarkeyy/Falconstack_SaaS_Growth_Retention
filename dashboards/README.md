Growth Sustainability Dashboard
Purpose: show whether revenue/customer growth is healthy or overly dependent on acquisition.

Core visuals:

Monthly MRR / ARR trend
New MRR vs churned MRR vs expansion MRR vs contraction MRR
NRR and GRR trend
Active customers over time
Customer growth rate vs churn rate
ARPU and LTV trend

Key message: “Growth rose into late 2024, but retention and churn pressure made it unsustainable.”

Churn Diagnosis Dashboard
Purpose: explain where churn happens and why.

Core visuals:

Monthly churn events and churned MRR
Churn reason breakdown
Churn reason by plan tier, industry, and referral source
Early churn rate by signup month
30-day and 90-day cohort retention
Churn after upgrade vs churn after downgrade
Refund amount by churn reason

Key message: “Late-2024 churn spike is driven by feature gaps, budget pressure, and support issues, with early-life churn becoming severe.”

Segment Investment Dashboard
Purpose: identify which segments deserve more investment, retention work, or caution.

Core visuals:

ARR share by industry, country, referral source, and plan tier
Churn rate by segment
ARR share vs churn rate scatterplot
Segment matrix: high value / low churn / high churn
Product usage depth and feature breadth by segment
Support burden by segment
Trial vs paid retention and ARR contribution

Key message: “Organic, Partner, Cybersecurity, EdTech, and FinTech look more defensible, while DevTools, Event-sourced customers, and some Enterprise cohorts need retention fixes before heavier investment.”

The overall story is:

Dashboard 1: Is growth healthy?
Dashboard 2: Why are customers leaving?
Dashboard 3: Where should we invest?
